# Outpost Legends Game Spec

## MVP Summary

Outpost Legends is a co-op survival defense simulator RPG where a small team defends an outpost against escalating enemy waves. Players choose a hero class, gather and spend scrap, build defenses, repair the base, and survive through a boss wave.

## Core MVP Pillars

- **Co-op survival:** Up to four players defend one shared outpost.
- **Hero roles:** Vanguard, Ranger, Engineer, and Medic provide clear team responsibilities.
- **Wave defense:** Five escalating waves lead into a boss wave.
- **Build phase decisions:** Players spend scrap on walls, turrets, and spike traps.
- **Server-owned rewards:** Scrap, progression, enemy defeats, and wave rewards are granted only by the server.

## MVP Exclusions

The first milestone intentionally excludes pets, rebirth, trading, PvP, multiple maps, monetization, and complex gear systems.
